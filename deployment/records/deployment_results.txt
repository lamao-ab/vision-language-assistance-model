  How 3.2 and 3.3 relate: the fixed analyzer's total (15.80 J) and net (8.67 J) are lower than the exact-window
  values (16.62 J, 9.02 J) because it sums only the samples inside each window (about 1.72 s of the 1.84 s marked
  window); its own self-check at the end of section 3.2 reports the same shortfall (15.80 vs 17.62 J = P_active x t).
  Power also stays above idle for more than a second after each window, so the net energy of a complete query is larger still.

====================================================================================================
4. KNOWN LIMITS OF THIS RECORD
====================================================================================================
  - Output token counts were not recorded; max_new_tokens (30, 64) is a limit, not the generated length.
  - Latency (live camera frames, both tasks) and energy (one fixed frame, caption task) are separate protocols.
  - Energy rests on one capture of 30 queries. The memory and latency measurements are repeated (2 and 3 runs).
  - The script versions that produced sections 1.3 and 1.4 are not yet in the repository.
  - tegrastats timestamps have one-second resolution; sub-second times are reconstructed by spreading samples evenly.
